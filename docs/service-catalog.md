# Managed service catalog

## Essential operations

- Monthly inventory and ownership review
- Budget and service-health reporting
- Patch, backup and monitoring coverage reports
- Prioritized change proposals
- Business-hours response

Indicative commercial model: USD 5,000–10,000 onboarding and USD 1,500–3,000 monthly.

## Managed Azure platform

- Continuous inventory
- Landing-zone and network operations
- Incident triage
- IaC change management
- Backup restore exercises
- Cost optimization backlog and verified-savings reporting

Indicative commercial model: USD 10,000–25,000 onboarding and USD 4,000–8,000 monthly.

## Hybrid operations

- Azure Arc onboarding for explicitly authorized servers
- Hybrid inventory and patch posture
- Cross-environment monitoring
- Connectivity and dependency management
- Recovery exercises

Indicative commercial model: USD 20,000–50,000 onboarding and USD 8,000–20,000 monthly.

These are positioning ranges, not quotes. Final pricing must account for resource count, ingestion,
coverage hours, SLA, data location, required licenses, integrations and professional liability.
Azure consumption and third-party licenses remain customer-paid unless the contract says otherwise.

## Recovery assurance

- Workload dependency and recovery contract
- RTO/RPO validation
- Isolated restore or test-failover plan
- Infrastructure and application assertions
- Recovery cost and observed-exposure report
- Review-gated remediation backlog

Indicative ranges: USD 2,500–7,500 for readiness assessment, USD 3,000–10,000 for a
single-workload restore proof and USD 10,000–30,000 for a mission-critical application drill.
Quarterly assurance begins around USD 2,000–8,000 monthly. These ranges exclude cloud consumption,
software licenses, around-the-clock coverage and production failover execution.
